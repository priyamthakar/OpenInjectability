### Friction-corrected force claims

From **search metadata alone**, **no paper can be certified** as reporting friction-corrected / fluid-only needle force.
Human full-text review should look for: empty-syringe friction subtraction, pressure transducer distal to stopper, or explicit separation of break-loose/glide friction from Hagen–Poiseuille fluid resistance.
Prior project assessment already flags that most OA “injection force” literature reports **total** glide force (includes stopper–barrel friction).

**None of the free-full-text hits in this batch claim (in the title) friction-corrected fluid force.** Whether any abstract/full text does so requires human reading; this agent does not fabricate that claim.
